The problem

Deposits inflate your P&L. Returns don't lie.

Put €500 in every month and your Total P&L climbs even when every one of your picks is flat, because the number counts your own cash as gains. Time-weighted return removes the cash flows and measures only what your investing decisions did — the same method funds are required to report.

What DEGIRO shows you
+21 448 €

Total P&L. Rises when you deposit, falls when you withdraw, and mixes both with actual performance. Useless for judging your own decisions.

What Sharpe shows you
+41.1%

Time-weighted return since inception, with the deposits stripped out — plus annualised return, max drawdown, beta, alpha and Sharpe ratio for whatever window you're looking at.

What you get

Six tabs of analysis DEGIRO doesn't give you.

Portfolio

One chart, the whole story

  • Cumulative or daily. The full curve since inception, or a bar per trading day to see which days made your year.
  • Benchmark overlays. S&P 500, Euro Stoxx 50 and Nasdaq 100, anchored to the day your portfolio actually started.
  • Trade markers so you can see how each buy and sell landed, and drag anywhere to zoom.
  • Positions that explain themselves: weight bars, a 30-day sparkline per holding, and click-to-expand detail with average cost, volatility and a full price chart.
Insights

Whether your diversification is real

  • Portfolio Pulse. Plain-language observations from your own figures — concentration, risk-adjusted trend, drawdown against your high-water mark.
  • Correlation matrix across every holding, with weighted average correlation and a principal-component breakdown. Six ETFs that all track the same index is not diversification.
  • Risk analysis. Daily Value at Risk at 90/95/99% confidence, plus stress tests against real historical crashes with per-position impact.
  • Rate My Portfolio. Five questions about your horizon and goals, then a grade against your objectives rather than a generic template.
Today

What moved, and what it cost you

  • Per-position daily change in % and €, with each holding's contribution to the day's return.
  • Three views of the same day: a table, a contribution waterfall, or a heatmap sized by weight and coloured by move.
  • Replay any past day — the whole tab rebuilt from your transaction history as it stood that morning.
  • Daily stats: winning streak, best and worst day of the month, average daily return and win rate.
Dividends & Tax

Income you'd otherwise have to total by hand

  • Every payment DEGIRO ever credited you, totalled all-time and over the trailing 12 months, with a monthly chart and your yield on cost.
  • Expected next payments, read from the cadence of your own history — labelled as an estimate, because issuers announce dividends, not extensions.
  • Realized P&L from closed positions on FIFO cost basis, and the currency effect on your non-EUR holdings.
  • Tax tables for the Netherlands, France, Germany and Belgium, built from your own history and exportable as a PDF.
Privacy & security

You should be paranoid about this. So am I.

An extension that reads your brokerage account deserves scrutiny. Here is exactly what Sharpe does and doesn't do — and because the source is public, you don't have to take my word for it.

Where it runs
Entirely in your browser. There is no backend, no database and no account to create. Your portfolio data is never transmitted anywhere.
Account access
Read-only. Sharpe issues GET requests to DEGIRO's own reporting endpoints — the same ones the website calls. It cannot place, modify or cancel an order.
Credentials
Never seen or stored. Sharpe uses the session you already have open; it never asks for your DEGIRO login.
Dependencies
Vanilla JavaScript. No npm packages, no frameworks, no minified third-party bundles — nothing that could turn into a supply-chain problem.
Architecture
Manifest V3, which means the browser itself forbids remote code execution. The extension cannot download and run new code after you install it.
Data at rest
Cached locally so the dashboard opens instantly, and cleared automatically after 24 hours.
Verify it yourself
Every file in the public repository is exactly what ships to the Chrome Web Store. Clone it, read it, load it unpacked.

Install

Two minutes, no signup.

  1. Install from the Chrome Web Store — Sharpe: Portfolio Analytics for DEGIRO.
  2. Pin it to your toolbar so it's one click away.
  3. Log in to DEGIRO as you normally would, at trader.degiro.nl or your local domain.
  4. Open the extension. The first sync takes 20–30 seconds while it fetches your price history; after that the dashboard opens instantly.

Prefer to run it from source? Clone the repository, open chrome://extensions, enable Developer mode and choose Load unpacked. Firefox support is planned.
