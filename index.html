Portfolio growth and investment return are different.

You start with €10,000. Your investments gain 10%, then you add €5,000. Your portfolio is now worth €16,000—but your investment return is still 10%.

Time-weighted return accounts for deposits and withdrawals so you can compare investment performance across periods.
